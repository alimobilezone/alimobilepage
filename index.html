About Ali Mobile Zone

A repair counter built on getting it right the first time.

Ali Mobile Zone started as a single repair bench in Lahore and grew on word of mouth — customers sending friends and family because their phone came back working properly, with no shortcuts. We still work that way: one technician, one phone, one careful repair at a time.

  • Mission

    Repair it properly, or tell you honestly why not

    We diagnose before we quote, use parts we'd put in our own phones, and never replace more than what's actually broken.

  • Values

    Transparency at every step

    You'll know the fault, the part being used, and the price before any work starts — no surprises when you come to collect it.

  • Difference

    Trained hands, tested results

    Every repair is bench-tested before handover, and backed by a written warranty — most shops in the market don't offer either.

Battery Replacement
